The Lani Stylez Story

Hi, I'm Lani — a passionate hair stylist rooted in the heart of Hamilton, Ontario. With over 8 years of experience, I've dedicated my craft to helping clients look and feel their absolute best.

I believe hair is the ultimate form of self-expression. Whether you're after a bold transformation or a subtle refresh, I bring artistry, precision, and genuine care to every single appointment.

Trained in Hamilton and continuing education across North America, I stay current with the latest techniques — from lived-in colour to intricate protective styles that celebrate every texture.

Booking Policy

  • A $40 non-refundable deposit is required to secure your appointment. The remaining balance is due in cash on the day of your visit.
  • Please provide at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. Cancellations made within 24 hours of your appointment will forfeit the deposit.
  • No-shows (no cancellation, no contact) will forfeit the full deposit. Clients with 2 or more no-shows will be required to prepay in full for future bookings.
  • Please arrive on time. If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, your appointment may be shortened to stay on schedule for other clients.
  • If Lani needs to reschedule your appointment, you will receive priority rebooking at no additional cost.
